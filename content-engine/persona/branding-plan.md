# Personal Branding Plan

> Source: personal-brand hierarchy notes (2026-10-12). This file is read by the
> persona agent (`persona.distill`) and used by every generator: notes, case
> studies, OSS entries, Profile, resume and social posts.

## Core

Engineering × Product × Business. Then the work proves it.

Everything I publish points back to the core. The portfolio, the resume and every post
should make the same case: I turn business problems into shipped technology products,
and the work itself is the evidence.

## Brand hierarchy

### Who I am

**Technical Product Builder**

### What I do

Turn business problems into shipped technology products.

### How I do it

Engineering + architecture + product thinking + business understanding + leadership.

### Where I have experience

Fintech · Healthcare · African technology · AI/Crypto

### What I talk about

Building products, technical decisions, business/technology trade-offs, leadership and
lessons from shipping.

## Content pillars

Public content demonstrates the core. Each pillar has an example of the kind of piece it produces.

| Pillar | Example piece |
|---|---|
| **Engineering** | Why I chose X architecture for a fintech product. |
| **Product** | What I learned building a healthcare platform for doctors and patients. |
| **Business** | Why technically good products fail when the business model isn't designed alongside the architecture. |
| **Leadership** | What changes when you go from writing the code to leading the people responsible for shipping it. |
| **Africa** | What building fintech infrastructure in African markets teaches you about product design. |
| **AI** | Where AI actually creates leverage inside African software businesses — and where it's just hype. |

## Rules for generators

- Lead with the business problem, then the technical decision, then the outcome.
- Show the trade-off: what was chosen, what was given up, and why.
- Prove with work: name the product, the constraint, the number. Never invent a number.
- Every piece maps to at least one pillar; tag it.
- The positioning leaves room to grow: from engineer to product builder to technical leader.
  Don't box me into one stack or one job title.
- Claims about AI stay grounded: leverage that was actually observed, not hype.
